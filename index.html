About Me

I am a certified orientation and mobility specialist working as an independent contractor in Washington, DC. I have worked with people of all ages and skill levels, and I am available for private contracting with individuals, school districts, universities, and rehabilitation centers.

Please note that I use a mobility scooter for extended outdoor sessions. This does not affect my ability to provide full O&M training.

How I Can Help

  • White Cane Training

    If you have recently experienced a change in your vision, I can help you learn to use a white cane for safe and confident travel.

  • Dog Guide Services

    I assist with dog guide school applications, assessments, and patterning your dog guide.

  • Assistive Technology

    I love technology and can help you get the most out of your smartphone — iPhone or Android — and the amazing travel apps available today.

  • DC Area Orientation

    If you are new to the area, I can help you orient to your new neighborhood and to our Metrobus and Metrorail systems.
